About Crust & Co.

Honoring the traditional craft of slow sourdough baking.

Our Baking Philosophy

At Crust & Co., we believe simple ingredients make extraordinary food. Our flour is stone-milled locally, and our sourdough cultures have been nurtured over many years[cite: 4].

Every single loaf undergoes a 24-hour cold fermentation process, creating rich flavors, a chewy texture, and a golden crispy crust[cite: 4].
